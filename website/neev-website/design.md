# NEEV Design System

## Brand
- Name: NEEV (means "Foundation" in Hindi)
- Tagline: "The Foundation of Confident Parenting"
- Company: Ecoraa Vision Pvt. Ltd.

## Colors
- Primary Forest Green: #1F4D2C
- Deep Green: #0F3019
- Soft Sage: #7A9E6A
- Warm Cream: #FAF3E0
- Ivory: #FFF9EB
- Accent Gold: #C9A84C
- Amber Glow: #E8B547
- Soft Pink: #F2D4C8
- Deep Brown: #3D2B1F
- Warm Grey-Brown: #6B6555

## Typography
- Headings: Playfair Display (serif, 700)
- Italic taglines: Cormorant Gararant (italic, 500)
- Body: DM Sans (clean, modern)
- Feel: Premium, organic, warm

## Visual Language
- Nature-inspired: trees, roots, growth, leaves
- Floating particles, fireflies, falling leaves
- Soft glowing orbs, organic shapes
- Glass morphism cards with warm tones
- No sharp corners — everything rounded/organic
- Premium ambient lighting effects

## Animation Philosophy
- Spring physics, no linear easing
- Everything grows/blooms — nothing just appears
- Stagger reveals for lists
- Parallax depth on scroll
- Warm glow on hover states

## Layout
- Full viewport sections
- Generous negative space
- Asymmetric compositions
- Phone mockups with 3D tilt and float animation
